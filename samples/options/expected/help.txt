Description:
  Reads a JSONC settings document, checks it and prints what it bound.

Usage:
  options [<input>] [options]

Arguments:
  <input>  The settings document to read. [default: inputs/settings.jsonc]

Options:
  --schema        Print the JSON schema of the settings instead of reading a document.
  -?, -h, --help  Show help and usage information
  --version       Show version information
